The audio is human-recorded Mandarin, as identified by its source; it is not TTS. The
GIFs are published stroke-order animations, not generated stroke paths. Preserve this
notice and the source/license links when redistributing the pack. The proposed lesson
notes also include per-word attribution so a future approved release can display it.

Hanzi Writer data was investigated but is not incorporated. No Arphic-derived files or
TTS provider outputs are included. The metadata receipts retain the original source
credit information; source pages document additional contributors and licensing choices.
